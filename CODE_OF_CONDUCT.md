# Code of conduct

densa-rs projects follow the [Rust Code of Conduct](https://www.rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct):
be friendly and patient, welcoming and respectful, and keep discussion on the work.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through a repository's **Security**
tab (**Report a vulnerability** creates a private channel), or by private message to a
maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.
